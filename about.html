About

Two West Virginians who like markets more than most people find reasonable.

We started Mountain State Money because we kept fielding the same questions from friends, colleagues, and students: what's a bond, should I max out my Roth, is now a bad time to invest. This site is where we write the answers down properly, once, for anyone.

What this is

Free articles and short videos on investing basics — savings, stocks, bonds, retirement accounts, and risk. Written in plain language, aimed at people just getting started.

What this isn't

Personalized financial advice. We're not registered investment advisors, and nothing here should be read as a recommendation for your specific situation. We're not affiliated with any brokerage, insurer, or advisory firm.

Educational content only. Not personalized investment advice. Always do your own research or talk to a licensed professional before making financial decisions.
