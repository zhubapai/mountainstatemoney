Why this exists

We're two West Virginians who've spent years studying markets for ourselves — one teaches math, the other's just as deep into portfolios and bonds. We kept getting asked the same questions by friends and colleagues, so we started answering them here.

Everything on this site is free and general — the kind of thing you'd want to know before your first 401(k) meeting, not a pitch for a specific account or product.

Educational content only. Nothing here is personalized investment advice.
